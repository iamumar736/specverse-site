# SpecVerse data attribution

## DeviceFarmer open device database

The automatically imported Android device records in `imported.json` and corresponding records in `catalog.json` are adapted from **DeviceFarmer/stf-device-db**:

- Source: https://github.com/DeviceFarmer/stf-device-db
- Data license: **Creative Commons Attribution–ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0)** — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
- The upstream selection and arrangement is CC BY-SA 4.0. The factual retail device identifiers originate in the Google Play Supported Devices list, which Google publishes without a stated license. Hardware fields, where present, derive from the LineageOS Wiki (CC BY-SA 3.0); release dates derive from Wikidata (CC0).
- Changes made by SpecVerse: filtering named brands, excluding obvious tablets/televisions/wearables/test hardware, normalizing model identities, merging duplicate carrier/model identifiers, and adding incomplete-data labels.
- SpecVerse's adapted DeviceFarmer-derived catalog records are shared under CC BY-SA 4.0. Attribution and license notices must accompany redistribution of these records.

**Important:** The source covers many Android devices, not every phone ever manufactured. Names may include regional variants; imported specifications may refer to only one model variant. Missing fields are not invented or inferred.

## Other information

Legacy model names sourced from [Unihertz](https://www.unihertz.com/pages/business), [AGM](https://www.agmmobile.com/collections/rugged-smartphones), [iFixit Microsoft Lumia](https://www.ifixit.com/Device/Microsoft), and [PhoneArena's Essential Phone listing](https://www.phonearena.com/phones/Essential-Phone_id10548) are separate source-attributed factual model identities.

Manufacturer specifications and pricing information added to other records retain their per-record source URL. This attribution does not assert endorsement by any manufacturer or publisher.
